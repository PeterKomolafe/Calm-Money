# IDEA-01: Revive

Parked on 16 October 2026. Come back to it after the Monday Morning Download has run for 4 to 6 weeks.

## The idea
A product for non-technical creators who have a mailing list they no longer use. It cleans the list, wins back cold contacts, then runs a weekly email for them with one-tap approval. Built on what we set up for Peter's Monday Morning Download.

## What the customer gets
1. Connect their email service (Klaviyo, Kit, Mailchimp) with a normal login. No API keys.
2. One-off setup: topic, voice, audience, and their podcast, YouTube or blog links.
3. Day-one list clean-up: remove duplicates and unsubscribes, then send cold contacts a "Can I keep emailing you?" invite. This protects their sender reputation.
4. Every week: a drafted email (and article if they have a site), sent to them by email or WhatsApp with one "Approve" button.

## Why it could work
- Two common problems in one: no time to write, and a list gone cold.
- The edge is "revive your dead list", not "AI newsletter". Most tools skip the clean-up.

## What it takes
- Hosted app: logins, email-service connections, weekly scheduler, AI drafting, approval by email or WhatsApp.
- First version: about 2 to 3 months with one developer, plus hosting, AI and support costs.

## Risks to answer before building
- Will disengaged creators actually press "Approve" each week? Removing approval moves responsibility for their content to us.
- Who checks facts outside Peter's own field (finance)?
- Beehiiv and Kit are moving into AI drafting.
- Support load: every customer's setup breaks differently.

## Cheapest test
Run it by hand as a done-for-you service for 3 to 5 creators at a monthly fee. If they stay for 3 months, build the product.

## Proof so far (Peter's own account, 16 Oct 2026)
- 22 Klaviyo lists, 4,495 list entries, 2,190 people, 2,003 emailable.
- Main List and Main List V2 merged into one list of 1,599 emailable people.
- 404 people on other lists sent an opt-in invite.
- Weekly prep runs every Friday; Peter approves with one line.
