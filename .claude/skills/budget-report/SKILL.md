# Autumn Budget consumer report (Salary Finance)

Recreated on 26 Oct 2026 from the "Autumn Budget consumer report" space on Peter's desktop Mac (his screenshot of its instructions).

## The job
- Peter is writing the Autumn Budget summary for Salary Finance, who pay him for his expertise and his personal take. Budget day is **28 October 2026**.
- Turn the official Budget document into Peter's summary, using the template.

## Deadlines (UK time; Peter is in Dubai, 4 hours ahead, so give both)
- First draft by **noon on 29 October** (4pm Dubai).
- Salary Finance feedback by **6pm** that day (10pm Dubai).
- Final version by **noon on 30 October** (4pm Dubai).

## What gets delivered
- **Part 1, the employee summary and blog:** 600 to 800 words. A headline, a short intro, "Three things to watch", six topics, other measures, key dates and where to get help.
- **Part 2, employers:** the top 3 to 5 items with 2 to 3 bullets each. Separate, not counted in the word count.
- **Part 3, sources for fact-checking:** each fact with its start date and Budget document page. Separate, not counted.

## Rules for the writing
- Plain UK English, short sentences, no jargon, speaking to "you". Many readers don't speak English as a first language.
- Warm, never preachy, in line with Salary Finance's tone of voice.
- Confirmed announcements only, and every change gets its start date.
- Guidance, not advice: "you may want to", never "you should".
- Neutral: no political commentary and no view on whether a change is good or bad.
- House rules: "Peter" not "Pete", and no em dashes.

## Peter's personal take
- It gets its own space: "Three things to watch" plus a one-line watch-out per topic.
- The watch-outs cover timing, behaviour and what's easy to miss, not policy judgements.
- Any examples you write are placeholders. On the day, Peter's words replace them.

## How to work on the day
- Extract the Budget text and read the **whole Policy Decisions chapter**. Don't rely on keyword search alone: in the dry run that missed the two-child limit date.
- Log each fact with its page, check page numbers, and flag anything not stated instead of guessing.
- Draft for Peter to review before anything goes to Salary Finance. Never send anything to Salary Finance yourself.
- Show drafts as an artifact or a Claude Doc Peter can edit.
