# Peter's HQ

The dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/WkA6GQixmitLsqjdkxpyYt

The page reads everything from its own database, so updating numbers never needs a republish. Write with the `ArtifactData` tool on that url.

- `metrics/current`: one document with `updated`, `sales`, `bookings`, `list`, `youtube`, `instagram`, `upcoming`.
- `todos/<id>`: `{text, done, created, order}`. Peter ticks and adds these on the page.
- `deals/<id>`: `{name, kind ("Brand deal" or "Work"), value, currency ("GBP" or "AED"), stage, created, order}`. The Pipeline tile. Stages: Lead, In talks, Agreed, Delivering, then Done or Lost (hidden). Peter adds these on the page; add or move one when he asks. Never invent a value.
- `history/<YYYY-MM-DD>`: one small document per day `{youtube, instagram, list}`, used for growth figures.

## Morning refresh (7am, every day)

1. Stripe and Klaviyo: `python3 .claude/scripts/hq_numbers.py` prints `updated`, `sales`, `bookings`, `list`, `queue` (scheduled emails, plus recent drafts flagged as not scheduled) (including `list.lists`, every Klaviyo list with its size, for the mailing list card). It also prints `calmMoney` (`{total, payments, payers, thisMonth, prevMonth, prevLabel}`): Calm Money revenue from Stripe since Jan 2026, made of founder payments from the learning platform, website checkouts and coaching payment-plan instalments. If it's missing (Stripe refused part of it), keep yesterday's `calmMoney`.
2. YouTube (exact count): the public `subscriberCount` is rounded by YouTube (77,200), so don't use it for the card. Instead use `vidiq_channel_analytics` on `UChG3TKNbIc7PzOqd2S31RdA` with metrics subscribersGained and subscribersLost, dimension `day`, from the 1st of this month to today (5 credits). `subs` = `youtube.base.total` (net subscribers up to the end of `youtube.base.through`, read from `metrics/current`) + this month's gained minus lost. Save `youtube: {subs, gained30, asOf (the last day with data, yyyy-mm-dd; it lags 2 to 3 days), asOfText ("16 Oct"), base}`. On the first refresh of a new month, first move the base forward: add last month's net (dimension `month`, or the sum of its days) to `base.total` and set `base.through` to last month. If the analytics call fails, fall back to `vidiq_user_channels` and say so in the note. Sanity check: `subs` should round to the public figure; if it is more than 1,000 away, keep yesterday's figure and say so. `gained30` = today minus the `history` document from 30 days ago; if there's no history that old yet, keep the current value.
3. Instagram: `vidiq_ig_profile` for `conversationofmoney` (5 credits) gives followers and posts; save both (`instagram: {followers, posts, note}`). Set `instagram.note` to the change since yesterday's `history` document (for example "+40 since yesterday"); with no history, "Growth shows from tomorrow".
4. Zoho Books (the "ZohoBooks_Mine" connector, read-only): `ZohoBooks_list_invoices` with `organization_id: ""` (blank uses Peter's organisation, Conversation of Money Ltd), `status: "unpaid"`, `per_page: 200`. Build `invoices: {total, count, overdueCount, overdueTotal, items}`, where each item is `{customer, number, amount, due, dueText, overdue}` (`due` is the due date as yyyy-mm-dd; the page uses it for the "Needs you" list). `amount` is the invoice's `balance`. `dueText` is "Overdue N days (was due 8 Oct)" when overdue, else the due date ("Wed 21 Oct"). Sort with overdue first, then by due date. Never call any Zoho tool that changes anything. If any are added to the connector, leave them alone.
   UAE: the same again with the "ZohoBooksUAE" connector and `organization_id: "938313218"` (Moneytelligence Services FZCO, invoices in AED), saved as `invoicesUAE` in the same shape. Keep AED separate: never convert it or add it to the £ total. That login also holds an organisation called "Conversation of Money Ltd" (939976654), which isn't the live UK books. Ignore it.
4a. Invoices paid this month: `ZohoBooks_list_invoices` with `status: "paid"`, `last_modified_time` from the 1st of the month (an invoice dated last month can be paid this month, so don't filter on the invoice date), `per_page: 200`, for UK (`organization_id: ""`) and UAE (`"938313218"`). Keep only invoices whose `last_payment_date` is in this month. Save `invoicesPaid: {month: "yyyy-mm", total (UK, £), count, uaeTotal (AED, never converted), items: [{customer, number, amount, paid}]}`. The Revenue card shows Stripe sales plus `invoicesPaid.total` as "received this month". Leave out invoices to Peter's own companies (customer "Conversation of Money Ltd" or "Moneytelligence"): those are transfers between his businesses, not revenue. Never count anything twice: if an invoice was paid through Stripe, it is already in `sales`.
4b. YouTube analytics, **Mondays only** (10 credits; YouTube's figures lag 2 to 3 days anyway): `vidiq_channel_analytics` for channel `UChG3TKNbIc7PzOqd2S31RdA`, once for the 28 days ending 2 days ago and once for the 28 days before that, with metrics views, engagedViews, subscribersGained, subscribersLost, estimatedRevenue. Save `youtube28: {from, to, toText ("18 Oct"), views, engaged, engagedPrev, gained, lost, net, netPrev, revenue, revenuePrev, currency: "USD"}`. Revenue comes back in US dollars. Peter wants it shown in pounds: look up that day's USD to GBP mid-market rate (a web search; if two sources disagree, use the lower figure), then save `revenueGbp` and `revenuePrevGbp` (dollars times the rate, to 2 decimals), `fxRate`, and `fxText` such as "about £0.75 per $1 on 19 Oct". Keep `revenue` and `revenuePrev` in dollars too, because the page shows the dollar figure as the source. Compare engaged views, not views: YouTube changed how it counts views on 27 Aug 2026, so raw views across that date aren't comparable. On other days, leave `youtube28` as it is.
5. Read `metrics/current` and write it back with `update`, pinned to the version you read: the new `updated`, `sales`, `bookings`, `list`, `youtube: {subs, gained30, asOf, asOfText, base}`, `instagram: {followers, note}`, `invoices`, `invoicesUAE`, `invoicesPaid`, `queue`, `today`, `calmMoney`, and `youtube28` on Mondays. Drop `upcoming` entries whose date has passed. Keep the rest.
6. Chase to-dos: for each unpaid invoice (UK or UAE) that is now overdue, make sure `todos/chase-<invoice number>` exists. If it doesn't, `set` it as `{text: "Chase <customer> for <amount> (invoice <number>, N days overdue)", done: false, created: <now ms>, order: 0, auto: "chase"}`. If it exists, don't touch it (Peter may have ticked it). When an invoice is no longer in the unpaid list and its chase to-do is still open, `update` it to `done: true`.
7. Today and meeting follow-ups (Wispr Flow connector): `search_calendar_events` for today (since local midnight, until the next). Save `today: {connected: true, events: [{time: "10:00", title}], next: [{when: "Tue 20 Oct, 2pm", title}]}` in UAE time (Asia/Dubai, UTC+4, no clock changes; "today" means today in the UAE), where `next` is the next 3 events in the coming 7 days (`list_upcoming_meetings` with window_hours 168). The calendar is Peter's Microsoft calendar, linked through Wispr Flow. If it returns nothing and no past meetings exist either, save `today: {connected: false, events: []}`: Peter hasn't linked a calendar yet. Then `search_meetings` for yesterday. For each recorded meeting, `get_meeting` and read the next steps in its summary. Add each action that is Peter's as `todos/mtg-<meeting id>-<n>` (`{text: "<action> (from <meeting title>)", done: false, created, order: 1, auto: "meeting"}`), only if that id doesn't exist yet. Skip actions that belong to other people.
8. `set` `history/<today>`.
9. Stop. Don't message Peter unless something failed. If a source fails, keep yesterday's figure and say so in that tile's note ("Stripe didn't answer, figure from yesterday").

## Rules

- Numbers come only from the sources above. Never estimate or round up. Payments under £1 are test payments and are not counted.
- Calm Money members are on the learning platform, which isn't connected. Don't show a figure for it unless Peter gives one.
- Dates in UK style ("Tue 3 Nov"). No em dashes. Always "Peter".
- To-dos: add or change them when Peter asks, or when a session agrees a task with him. Never delete one he added without being asked.
- Never send emails, change Stripe, change Klaviyo or change Zoho Books from this skill. It only reads them.

## The Agenda card

The page shows one Agenda card (Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later) built from `agenda`: a list of `{date: "yyyy-mm-dd", time: "HH:MM" or "", title, kind: "meeting" | "send" | "date"}` in UAE time (Asia/Dubai, UTC+4), because Peter reads his day in the UAE. Calendar times arrive in UTC: add 4 hours. Email send times and the 3-Night nights are set in UK time, so convert them too, and for a UK-timed event add the UK time to the title, e.g. "3-Night Money Plan, Night 1 (7:30pm UK)". Also save `agendaTz: "UAE time"` (the card shows it). Include every calendar event in the window: on 20 Oct "Meeting with Kula" (22 Oct) was missing. Each morning rebuild it from three sources:
- meetings from the calendar for the next 14 days (`search_calendar_events`), with kind "meeting";
- scheduled emails from `queue` (not drafts), with kind "send" and a short title such as "3-Night launch email 3 sends" or "Pre-work email sends";
- key dates from `upcoming`, with kind "date".
Drop anything in the past. Don't list the same thing twice. `today` and `upcoming` stay in the data, but the page no longer shows them directly.
