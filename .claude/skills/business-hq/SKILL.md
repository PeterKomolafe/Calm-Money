# Peter's HQ

The dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/WkA6GQixmitLsqjdkxpyYt

The page reads everything from its own database, so updating numbers never needs a republish. Write with the `ArtifactData` tool on that url.

- `metrics/current`: one document with `updated`, `sales`, `bookings`, `list`, `youtube`, `instagram`, `upcoming`.
- `todos/<id>`: `{text, done, created, order}`. Peter ticks and adds these on the page.
- `deals/<id>`: `{name, kind ("Brand deal" or "Work"), value, currency ("GBP" or "AED"), stage, created, order}`. The Pipeline tile. Stages: Lead, In talks, Agreed, Delivering, then Done or Lost (hidden). Peter adds these on the page; add or move one when he asks. Never invent a value.
- `history/<YYYY-MM-DD>`: one small document per day `{youtube, instagram, list}`, used for growth figures.
- `growth/<yyyy-mm>`: one document per month for the "Growth, month by month" section, which compares every metric with the month before (this month on pace). Shape: `{month, label ("Oct"), partial (true for the current month), daysIn, daysSoFar, ytDaysSoFar, asOf, ytAsOf, revenue: {stripe, stripeCount, invoices, total}, calmMoney, bookings, list: {joined, end}, yt: {subsEnd, gained, lost, net, views, engaged, revenueUsd, fx, revenueGbp, watchHours}, ig: {followers}, ytPace, ytSettled (current month only)}`. History from Jan 2026 is already in.

Save `youtube: {subs, public, exact, note, gained30, asOf (the last day with Analytics data, yyyy-mm-dd), asOfText ("16 Oct"), base}`. On the first refresh of a new month, first move the base forward: add last month's net (dimension `month`, or the sum of its days) to `base.total` and set `base.through` to last month. If the analytics call fails, keep yesterday's `exact` and say so in the note. `gained30` = today's `subs` minus the `history` document from 30 days ago; until there's history that old, use `vidiq_channel_stats` (5 credits; its `dailyStats` give the public count 30 days ago).

   UAE: the same again with the "ZohoBooksUAE" connector and `organization_id: "938313218"` (Moneytelligence Services FZCO, invoices in AED), saved as `invoicesUAE` in the same shape. 4a. Invoices paid this month: `ZohoBooks_list_invoices` with `status: "paid"`, `last_modified_time` from the 1st of the month (an invoice dated last month can be paid this month, so don't filter on the invoice date), `per_page: 200`, for UK (`organization_id: ""`) and UAE (`"938313218"`). YouTube, last 28 days against the 28 days before (the comparison YouTube Studio shows; the Growth section's YouTube tile uses it), **every day**: `vidiq_channel_analytics` for `UChG3TKNbIc7PzOqd2S31RdA` with no dimension, once for the 28 days ending on the latest day Analytics returns and once for the 28 days before that (10 credits), with metrics views, engagedViews, estimatedMinutesWatched, estimatedRevenue, subscribersGained, subscribersLost. ## Rules

- Numbers come only from the sources above. Never estimate or round up. Payments under £1 are test payments and are not counted.
- Calm Money members are on the learning platform, which isn't connected. Don't show a figure for it unless Peter gives one.
- Dates in UK style ("Tue 3 Nov"). No em dashes. Always "Peter".
- To-dos: add or change them when Peter asks, or when a session agrees a task with him. Never delete one he added without being asked.
- Never send emails, change Stripe, change Klaviyo or change Zoho Books from this skill. It only reads them.

## The Agenda card

The page shows one Agenda card (Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later) built from `agenda`: a list of `{date: "yyyy-mm-dd", time: "HH:MM" or "", title, kind: "meeting" | "send" | "date"}` in UAE time (Asia/Dubai, UTC+4), because Peter reads his day in the UAE. Calendar times arrive in UTC: add 4 hours. Email send times and the 3-Night nights are set in UK time, so convert them too, and for a UK-timed event add the UK time to the title, e.g. "3-Night Money Plan, Night 1 (7:30pm UK)". Also save `agendaTz: "UAE time"` (the card shows it). Include every calendar event in the window: on 20 Oct "Meeting with Kula" (22 Oct) was missing. Each morning rebuild it from three sources:
- meetings from the calendar for the next 14 days (`search_calendar_events`), with kind "meeting";
- scheduled emails from `queue` (not drafts), with kind "send" and a short title such as "3-Night launch email 3 sends" or "Pre-work email sends";
- key dates from `upcoming`, with kind "date".
Drop anything in the past. Don't list the same thing twice. `today` and `upcoming` stay in the data, but the page no longer shows them directly.
